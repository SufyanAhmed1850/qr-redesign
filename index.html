Design spec

  • AssanPay theme (taken from the receipt palette in ReceiptRenderer): white backdrop, eye rings in ink #251200, and data in ink-soft #5A3818 mixed with burnt orange #B8651E. The logo stays brand orange #E09145.
  • Why not brand orange for the modules: #E09145 on a light background is only about 2.1:1 contrast, and many scanners fail below about 3:1. The burnt orange gets about 4:1 on white and still reads as AssanPay.
  • Finder eyes: a 7-module ring with a 3-module centre; the light ring between them stays empty, so scanners still see the 1:1:3:1:1 ratio.
  • Fluid style: neighbouring modules merge into single shapes. Outer corners are fully rounded where both sides are open, inside corners get a small curve, and the corner eyes are rounded squares. All shapes are brown #5A3818, the same as production.
  • Dots style: every dark module is drawn as its own round dot (92% of the cell), the corner eyes are rounded squares, and there is no texture.
  • Rings style: smaller dots (72% of the cell) leave more open space. The corner eyes are bullseyes, a thick ring around a solid circle. There's no texture.
  • Alignment patterns: required by the QR standard from version 2 up; they let scanners correct tilt and curvature. Drawn as a small ring and dot that match the eyes. Any that would sit under the logo is left out.
  • Backdrop: plain white fills the whole image in every theme, with an empty quiet zone of 4 modules. Nothing is drawn outside the code area.
  • Logo: the AssanPay mark (the same path as ReceiptLogoPath) fills 92% of a rounded-square clearing in the centre. The clearing's corners are as round as the eyes' outer corners (2.3 of 7 modules). Nothing is drawn behind the logo: the clearing is just the code's squares left out wherever their centre falls inside it, and shapes that reach it end in a round cap, like everywhere else in the code. Error correction goes up to H (30% recovery), and only about 7% of the code is cleared. A code too small to fit the logo clear of the format information is drawn without it.
  • Server: production (QrGeneratorService) draws the fluid design with SkiaSharp at about 1 kpx as an opaque PNG. It still clears the logo with a white rounded square, so its clearing corners are trimmed along the arc rather than formed by whole rounded squares as here.
