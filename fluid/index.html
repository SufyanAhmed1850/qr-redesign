Design spec

  • Shapes: neighbouring modules merge into single shapes. Outer corners are fully rounded where both sides are open, and inside corners get a small curve.
  • Eyes: a 7-module rounded ring with a 3-module rounded centre. The light ring between them stays empty, so scanners still see the 1:1:3:1:1 ratio.
  • Alignment patterns: a small rounded ring and dot that match the eyes. Any that would sit under the logo is left out.
  • Logo: the AssanPay mark fills 92% of a rounded-square clearing as round as the eyes' outer corners. Nothing is drawn behind it: squares whose centre falls inside the clearing are left out, and shapes that reach it end in a round cap, like everywhere else in the code.
  • Error correction: H (30% recovery). The logo clears about 7% of the code. A code too small to fit the logo clear of the format information is drawn without it.
  • Background: transparent, including the 4-module quiet zone on every side. Place it on a light surface: scanners need dark shapes on a light background.
